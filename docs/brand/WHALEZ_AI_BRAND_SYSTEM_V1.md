# Whalez-AI Brand System V2

## Master identity

**Whalez-AI Ecosystem** is the umbrella/company identity.

**Whalez-AI** is the single intelligence identity within that ecosystem.

Models, agents, sub-agents, model providers, tools and runtimes are implementation mechanisms or delegated capabilities. They are not separate public AIs.

## Product family
- Whalez-AI Ecosystem — umbrella/system-of-record identity.
- Whalez-AI — intelligence, policy, reasoning and routing identity.
- DeltaAlpha-Trade-Pro — public financial operating platform.
- WhalezChain — native state, provenance and finality rail.
- WHZ / PTN / PRN — native asset identities within WhalezChain.

## Visual grammar
- Deep Field — `#0B1020`
- Soft Foreground — `#F8FAFC`
- Intelligence Cyan — `#6EE7F5`
- Execution / Market Blue — `#60A5FA`
- Native Asset Violet — `#A78BFA`

Shared geometry: rounded-square foundation, clean circular/triangular/receipt silhouettes, sparse node/line motifs, high-contrast foreground, restrained glow and glass surfaces.

## Product differentiation
- Whalez-AI Ecosystem — whale crest + intelligent core.
- DeltaAlpha-Trade-Pro — delta triangle + market/operating lines.
- WhalezChain — interlocking state/provenance geometry.
- WHZ — circular native-asset seal.
- PTN — structured trade-note geometry.
- PRN — receipt/finality-state geometry.

These differences provide product recognition without creating unrelated brands.

## Intelligence presentation

Use `Whalez-AI · <Capability>`.

Examples: `Whalez-AI · Intelligence Core`, `Whalez-AI · Execution`, `Whalez-AI · Security & Integrity`, `Whalez-AI · Governance & Compliance`, `Whalez-AI · Chain History`.

Do not present a model/provider/agent as a standalone public AI identity unless a deliberate product decision explicitly creates one.

## Asset truth rule

An asset mark communicates identity only. It is not evidence of external custody, liquidity, reserve backing, legal classification, mainnet finality, or external exchange listing.

## Private product boundary

Founder Console and other private authority surfaces may use the same family grammar, but their private access posture and internal-control details must remain outside the public application.
